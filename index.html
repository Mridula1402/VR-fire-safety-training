Pick what is burning. The guide shows which extinguishers are safe to use.

Using an extinguisher: PASS

Pull the safety pin.
Aim at the base of the fire, not the flames.
Squeeze the handle.
Sweep side to side until the fire is out.

Fight a fire only if it is small, the alarm is raised and you have an exit behind you. Otherwise, leave.

Emergency evacuation steps

  1. Raise the alarm. Shout and use the nearest alarm point.
  2. Call for help. Dial the fire service as soon as you are safe.
  3. Check doors first. Touch with the back of your hand. If it is hot, do not open it.
  4. Stay low in smoke. Smoke rises, and cleaner air is near the floor.
  5. Close doors behind you. This slows the spread of fire and smoke.
  6. Use stairs, never lifts. Lifts can stop between floors.
  7. Go to the assembly point. Report to the fire warden for a headcount.
  8. Never go back in. Leave rescue to the fire service.
